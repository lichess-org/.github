# Accessibility

Lichess is committed to providing everyone with an accessible and inclusive chess experience. 

This document explains our accessibility commitment, how contributors can help us uphold it, and how to report accessibility issues.

## Reporting accessibility issues
Accessibility at Lichess is not owned by a specific person or team, but rather the shared responsibility of everyone contributing to the project. If you have suggestions on how to make Lichess more inclusive and accessible, we would be delighted to hear from you. 

- Open issue
- send PR
- Send email
- Write on discord
